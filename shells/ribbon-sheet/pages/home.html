A blank Workspace

Nothing is here yet, on purpose. Every Page you add appears in the sidebar under Pages, and Ctrl K finds it.

  1. Write the Page's content in pages/<slug>.html.
  2. Create it: rt page create <slug> -t "Title" --canvas pages/<slug>.html.
  3. Patch it live: rt page use <slug>, then rt patch elements '<div id="hello">Hello</div>'.

The appearance (light, dark and the installed themes) is under Settings in the ribbon.
